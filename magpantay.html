A Child’s Journey Filled with Unforgettable Memories

by Magpantay, Sheena        |        July 23, 2025

    If you're in the Philippines, what comes to your mind when it is described as a tourist spot and a beautiful cold place? For me, it is Baguio. I went to Baguio, and it left happy memories with me when I was a kid. It was the farthest place I had ever been. There are many tourist spots, including Burnham Park, The Mansion, Mines View, and many more.

    First of all, I felt dizzy on the way there. You need to pass a few mountains to get to Baguio. It is also located on top of a mountain. I'm glad it was worth it when we finally reached Baguio.

    Before we explored Baguio, we went to church. This church is called Baguio Cathedral of Our Lady of Atonement. As Catholics, we always visit a church before exploring a place. My mom told me that I should make a wish because it's my first visit.

    I was excited to go inside SM Baguio because I like malls. Look at how excited I am as I jump high in this picture! My mom told me that SM Baguio has no aircon, and I was amazed. It only uses natural ventilation since the air is already naturally cold.

    At Mines View Park, I rode a horse for a picture. It was my first time riding a horse. I really liked the pink color of its hair, even though that isn’t its natural color. I was scared when I tried to ride because I thought I might fall off. You can see in the picture that I am smiling, but deep inside, I wanted to get down. I really enjoyed the view here because I could see that I was at the top of the mountain.

    You can't leave Baguio without getting pasalubongs or souvenirs. When we were at the market, we bought Baguio-printed beret hats and wore them throughout our Baguio exploration. We also bought sundot kulangot. I don’t know why it’s called that, since kulangot means “booger”. Maybe it’s because you pick the sugar-made food as if you’re picking a booger. You may not like the name, but sundot kulangot is tasty. It has a similar taste to inuyat, since they’re both made from sugar.

    I would definitely recommend this place if you're visiting Luzon, Philippines. I would also go back, since I really miss it. It's been 12 years since I last went. I’d love to try strawberry taho and visit the Strawberry Farm someday.
